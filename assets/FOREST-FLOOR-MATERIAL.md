# Shared forest floor

Original material generated with the built-in imagegen tool for terrain-surface.js. Reusable material, not a ground patch containing scene objects. Consumed as forest-floor-material.png.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: seamless ground-material texture for a detailed Nordic woodland pixel-art 2D game. A continuous flat expanse of forest floor ONLY: fine brown pine needles, muted olive moss patches, compact dark earth, sparse tiny gray pebbles and dry leaf fragments. Viewed from a shallow overhead oblique angle, allowing a landscape ground plane to be textured without obvious objects. Crisp fine pixel clusters, hand-painted high-detail pixel-art, muted olive/brown natural palette, soft uniform overcast daylight. Full-bleed rectangular texture, landscape 3:2. No horizon, no trees, no trunks, no large rocks, no plants standing upright, no hills, no water, no paths, no buildings, no directional beams, no shadows of branches, no vignette, no transparent background. All details small, evenly distributed but organically varied; avoid stripes, wave patterns and obvious repeating motifs.
