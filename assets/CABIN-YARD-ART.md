# Cabin yard art — 0.7.1

Final project asset: `assets/cabin-yard-props-final.png` (1536 × 1024 RGBA). Made with the built-in imagegen tool, not a CLI or extracted third-party game asset. Its four cutouts depict an old tractor, shed, fishing equipment and garden tools. The final alpha channel was checked; opaque-background drafts remain ignored under `work/` and are not shipped.

The existing Timbertrail cabin artwork supplied the style reference. The atlas is sampled through authored crop rectangles in `region-art.js`, using the shared terrain socket for contact. These are decoration only: no extra collisions, inventory or repair requirements. The doorway and spring remain separate interactions.

## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one transparent RGBA sprite atlas of four original Nordic cabin-yard props for Timbertrail. Image 1 is a STYLE reference only: match its painterly detailed pixel clusters, weathered timber, muted warm earthy palette, moss and side-view perspective. Create a 1536x1024 atlas with two columns and two rows, four clearly separated sprites inside their equal 768x512 quadrants, ample transparent padding, NO ground patches, cast shadows, backdrop, labels or grid lines. Upper-left: old modest 1950s farm tractor facing left in mostly side profile, faded muted red paint, rust, exposed engine, large rear rubber tyre and small front tyre, worn seat, small moss patches; not modern, no branding. Upper-right: little weathered timber tool shed, front with slight right-side perspective, simple sloped board roof, closed plank door with iron latch, a small neat stack of firewood under side eaves, moss at roof edges, low foundation, considerably humbler than a house. Lower-left: compact group of two wooden fishing rods leaning diagonally, oval landing net, wicker creel, small galvanized bait bucket and neatly coiled rope, prepared to lean against the cabin wall but DO NOT paint a wall. Lower-right: compact group of a rusty shovel and wooden rake leaning together, old axe resting against small chopping block and a few neatly split billets, NO wall. Every object complete and wholly within its quadrant; bottom contact clear, true transparent alpha including gaps between spokes/tools, no floating debris, no broad soil/grass mats, no people, no animals, no text. Restrained daylight from upper left. Pixel-art game sprites, not vector and not glossy 3D.

## Background-extraction prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Edit ONLY the background of this atlas. Remove every white and pale grey checkerboard square completely, including checkerboard visible between net strands, spokes, tool handles and other gaps. Output a genuinely transparent RGBA PNG with alpha=0 in all background pixels, NOT an image of a checkerboard. Keep the four props exactly as they are: identical placement, canvas dimensions 1536x1024, colors, detail, scale, shapes and pixel-art texture. No new props, no repainting or rearrangement, no cast shadows, no ground. Preserve the entire tractor, shed, fishing equipment and garden tools. Only extract background to transparency.

Transparency cleanup was repeated because the first outputs still depicted a checkerboard. The selected final RGBA output preserves clear gaps around the cutouts. Native Canvas scene snapshots were inspected; this does not replace an in-browser playtest.
